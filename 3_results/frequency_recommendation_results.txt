UNDERLOAD FREQUENCY RECOMMENDATION - closed-loop back-test
====================================================================

Rule: fit Pi = a - k*f on each well's own first 30 days, then reduce
frequency by (deficit + margin)/k whenever intake pressure falls more than
18 psi below what the current frequency should deliver.
Every recommendation is clipped to the well's own equipment-rated band; if
the floor binds, the well escalates to inspection instead.
